# Home Blueprint

DESIGN DIRECTION — MINIMAL, PREMIUM & EYE-CATCHING

Design BuildWise with a minimal editorial aesthetic combined with modern architectural design.

The interface should feel like a premium architecture, real-estate, and sustainability startup — clean enough to feel sophisticated, but visually distinctive enough to immediately catch attention.

Overall Style

Think:

Apple-level simplicity + premium architecture magazine + modern Pakistani home design.

Do NOT make the UI look like:

A generic SaaS dashboard

A typical AI chatbot

A banking app

An overly colorful startup template

A crowded architecture application

The design should have fewer elements, stronger hierarchy, larger typography, beautiful imagery, and intentional whitespace.

COLOR SYSTEM

Use a restrained palette.

Primary

Warm off-white / ivory background.

Text

Deep charcoal / almost-black.

Accent

One sophisticated muted green inspired by plants, sustainability, and architecture.

Use the accent color sparingly for:

Primary CTA

Active selections

Progress indicators

Important recommendation labels

Small interface highlights

Avoid using multiple bright colors.

The interface should feel calm and premium.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use a modern premium sans-serif typeface.

Large, confident headlines.

Example:

Build smarter.
Before you build.

Use generous line height and strong typography hierarchy.

Body text should remain short and highly readable.

Avoid excessive paragraphs.

Use:

Large display headlines

Medium section headings

Small supporting text

Clear labels

Bold numbers for important information

VISUAL LANGUAGE

Use large architectural photography as a visual storytelling element rather than filling every screen with cards.

Images should feature:

Modern Pakistani residential architecture

Courtyards

Natural light

Greenery

Contemporary homes

Roof spaces

Windows and ventilation

Warm natural materials

Photography should feel realistic, premium, and editorial.

Avoid generic Western suburban houses wherever possible.

HERO SECTION

Make the landing page visually powerful.

Use a split-screen or immersive hero layout.

Left side:

Small eyebrow:

HOME PLANNING, REIMAGINED

Large headline:

Build smarter.
Before you build.

Short description:

A smarter way to plan your home around your location, climate, lifestyle and future needs.

CTA:

Start Planning →

Secondary text link:

See how it works

Right side:

Large edge-to-edge architectural image of a beautiful contemporary Pakistani home.

Add a subtle floating glass/white information card over the image:

Your home, considered from every angle.

Small indicators:

Climate · Comfort · Energy · Future

Keep the overlay extremely minimal.

MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Use subtle animations throughout the app.

Examples:

Cards gently lift when selected

Buttons have smooth hover states

Progress bar animates between steps

Page transitions are smooth

Recommendation cards fade/slide in

Numbers animate when displayed

Selection states use subtle borders/background changes

Animations should feel premium and calm, never flashy.

ONBOARDING DESIGN

The questionnaire should NOT look like a long form.

Each step should feel like a focused conversation.

Example:

Top:

03 / 08

Small progress bar.

Large headline:

Where are you building?

Supporting text:

Your location helps us understand the conditions your home needs to respond to.

Then use large, elegant selection controls.

For example:

Karachi
Lahore
Islamabad
Other

Use cards with lots of whitespace.

Keep one primary question per screen whenever possible.

SELECTION CARDS

Selection cards should be minimalist.

Default:

White/off-white card
Thin subtle border
Small icon
Clear title
One-line description

Selected:

Use the muted green accent with a very subtle background tint and stronger border.

Do not use huge colorful illustrations.

QUESTIONNAIRE EXPERIENCE

At the top of every onboarding screen:

BuildWise

Step 04 of 08

Progress bar

At the bottom:

← Back

Continue →

Keep the CTA fixed/sticky on mobile where appropriate.

RECOMMENDATION DASHBOARD

This should be the visual highlight of the entire application.

Instead of displaying a boring grid of identical cards, create a premium editorial dashboard.

Header:

Good morning, Fizza.

Your home plan is taking shape.

Then show a compact profile summary:

Karachi · New Home · Modern · 5 People

PRIORITY INSIGHT

Create one large featured recommendation.

Example:

YOUR TOP CONSIDERATION

Heat & Ventilation

Karachi's climate makes natural airflow and heat management important areas to discuss during planning.

Button:

Explore recommendation →

Use a large architectural/ventilation image or subtle illustration beside the recommendation.

RECOMMENDATION CATEGORIES

Below the main insight, use asymmetric cards rather than identical boxes.

Example:

01

Orientation

How sunlight and positioning can influence comfort.

Explore →

02

Natural Light

Plan daylight into the spaces you use most.

Explore →

03

Energy

Consider insulation, shading and solar readiness.

Explore →

04

Water

Think about drainage and rainwater management early.

Explore →

05

Future

Plan spaces that can adapt as your family grows.

Explore →

Use different card sizes to create an editorial layout.

RECOMMENDATION DETAIL PAGES

Each recommendation page should feel like a mini architecture guide, not a technical report.

Example:

Large heading:

Make natural airflow work for you.

Small label:

CLIMATE & COMFORT

Large architectural image.

Then:

Why this matters

Short, easy-to-understand explanation.

Then a visual section:

Things worth discussing

01 Window placement
02 Cross ventilation
03 Shading
04 Room orientation

Then a highlighted insight:

BuildWise Tip

“Think about airflow during planning — not after construction.”

CTA:

Next consideration →

Keep it extremely clean.

Heading:

Your Home Profile

Show the user's information as elegant horizontal sections rather than dense cards.

LOCATION
Karachi

PROJECT
New Home

STYLE
Modern

FAMILY
5 people

TOP PRIORITIES
Natural Light · Ventilation · Energy

Button:

Edit Profile

FINAL PLAN SCREEN

Make this feel like a premium personalized report.

Header:

Your smarter home starts here.

Subheading:

A personalized planning guide based on your priorities.

Show:

5 things to discuss before you build

01 — Orientation
02 — Natural Ventilation
03 — Energy Efficiency
04 — Water & Drainage
05 — Future Expansion

Then a large CTA:

Share with your Architect →

Secondary:

Save My Plan

EMPTY SPACE & LAYOUT

Whitespace is a major part of the design.

Do not try to fill every part of the screen.

Use:

Large margins

Large image areas

Short copy

Strong typography

Carefully positioned elements

Every element should have a purpose.

The design should look expensive because it is restrained, not because it contains many visual effects.

MOBILE EXPERIENCE

The mobile version must feel intentionally designed, not simply compressed from desktop.

Use:

Large touch targets

Bottom sticky CTA

Horizontal scrolling cards where useful

Full-width imagery

Large typography

Minimal navigation

Comfortable spacing

On mobile, the app should feel almost like a premium editorial magazine that users can interact with.

SIGNATURE VISUAL ELEMENT

Create a subtle recurring architectural grid / floor-plan line motif throughout the application.

Use it very lightly in:

Backgrounds

Section transitions

Loading screen

Recommendation illustrations

It should become part of BuildWise's visual identity without making the interface busy.

FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The app should communicate this feeling:

“This is not another generic home-design app. This understands how I am actually planning my home.”

Prioritize:

Minimalism → strong typography → architectural imagery → whitespace → subtle interaction → personalization.

The final UI should be clean, eye-catching, premium, modern, and presentation-ready, while remaining simple enough for an ordinary Pakistani homeowner to understand immediately.
