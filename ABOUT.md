# BuildWise

**Build smarter. Before you build.**

BuildWise is a home-planning guide for people building or renovating in Pakistan. It helps homeowners think through climate, lifestyle, and future needs *before* construction — then turns those answers into a clear, shareable plan for an architect.

## The problem

Most people start building with a plot and a budget, but without a structured way to talk about orientation, ventilation, daylight, materials, or how the home should grow with the family. Important decisions get made late — or not at all — and comfort, energy, and adaptability suffer.

## What it does

BuildWise walks the user through a focused planning questionnaire (one question at a time), then generates a personalized planning guide covering topics such as:

- **Orientation** — sun path, plot facing, and room placement  
- **Ventilation** — natural airflow and heat management  
- **Sunlight** — usable daylight without overheating  
- **Materials** — climate-appropriate finishes and envelopes  
- **Spatial considerations** — family life, privacy, and outdoor use  
- **Design direction** — a coherent style that still works with the climate  

The result is a premium, editorial-style plan the user can save or share with their architect — not a technical report or a CAD tool.

## Who it’s for

Ordinary Pakistani homeowners planning a **new home**, **renovation**, or **redesign** in cities like Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and beyond — including plot sizes in familiar local terms (marla / kanal).

## How the app works

1. **Splash → Get started** — brand intro and entry into the app  
2. **Sign up / Sign in** — lightweight account flow  
3. **Home / Plan / Profile** — tabbed mobile experience  
4. **Planner** — questionnaire on project type, location, plot, climate, household, outdoor use, future needs, style, and priorities  
5. **Processing → Guides** — personalized topic recommendations  
6. **Final plan** — a shareable summary of what to discuss before building  

Answers and plans are saved locally so users can revisit and share their guide.

## Design intent

The product is meant to feel like a **premium architecture / real-estate / sustainability** experience — calm, editorial, and restrained — not a generic SaaS dashboard or chatbot. Visual language draws on contemporary Pakistani residential architecture: courtyards, natural light, greenery, and warm materials.

BuildWise is a climate- and lifestyle-aware home planning companion that turns a short questionnaire into a clear architect-ready guide for Pakistani homeowners.
