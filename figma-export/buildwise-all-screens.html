Guide

Climate & comfort

Make natural airflow work for you.

Why this matters

Karachi homes can hold heat long after sunset. Thoughtful openings, shaded edges and a clear path for air help rooms feel comfortable with less reliance on cooling — especially when Intense heat is a priority.

01 Window placement
